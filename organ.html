AVIATION ADMINISTRATIONS

Organizations responsible for aviation safety, regulation, and oversight worldwide.

North America

FAA

The Federal Aviation Administration regulates civil aviation in the United States. It manages air traffic control, aircraft certification, pilot licensing, airport standards, and aviation safety programs.

UNITED STATES

Europe

EASA

The European Union Aviation Safety Agency creates common aviation safety standards across Europe. It oversees aircraft certification, operational safety, and environmental requirements for European aviation.

EUROPEAN UNION

Asia

CAAC

The Civil Aviation Administration of China manages China's civil aviation system. It regulates airlines, airports, airworthiness, flight operations, and aviation safety.

CHINA
